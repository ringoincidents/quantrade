# Automatic public-reference handoff (verification only)

The existing public duty timer now runs a deterministic three-stage chain after each terminal collection attempt. The source collector belongs to Data; ASSURE (Risk) checks source identity, dates, series, input hash, authority flags, timestamps and Treasury spread arithmetic; COMPARE (Macro Research) classifies baseline, new observation, unchanged, same-period revision or older observation; HANDOFF (Operations) records completion or a bounded review proposal. All stages for a source attempt are committed atomically in local SQLite, with unique source/due/stage keys. A failed or corrupt input blocks descendants. A revision or older observation opens a PENDING_GOVERNANCE record, never an LLM call or trading action.

Daily, weekly (Monday KST), monthly and quarterly closure reports record source and handoff completion against four planned source jobs per calendar day, including weekends under the existing public-reference sampling plan. Missing coverage is explicitly reported. Reports close the most recent elapsed period on the next tick, not reconstruct every missing historical reporting interval after a long outage. They are immutable snapshots; late corrections and resolution of pending reviews require a separate governed path. These are operating summaries, not portfolio accounting, investment forecasts or approved annual plans.

The existing public-duty tick invokes the chain automatically and includes cumulative stage counts and pending review count in the local hourly report. The HQ bridge currently transmits only its existing status allowlist; it conveys the decision_requested flag but not new private handoff details. The real one-shot CI probe requires both sources and all six stages to complete. Same-process deterministic checks are not independent research review. Existing public source rights/finality limits remain. No raw data archive, provider budget gateway, remote approval, external AI call, broker access, paid infrastructure, merge or VPS/Railway deployment. Actual schedule activation remains gated by #150 and #142; reuse Holdings canonical Work/Authority rather than treating the local spool as authority.
