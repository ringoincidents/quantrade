# QuanTrade development guide v0.1 — 2026-11-28
Status: founder-directed draft on PR #141; does not supersede canonical vision, CLAUDE.md or existing authorization gates. No trading or production authorization.

## Purpose and design decisions
Founder supervises a private investment office, delegates routine work and intervenes where it creates value. Field operates actual investment workflows; Lab explores, tests and challenges investment methods; Office owns administration, reconciliation, review, cost, audit, research commissioning and promotion coordination. Independent risk retains veto/direct escalation. Investment philosophy can evolve on evidence, but capital limits, authority expansion and exceptions require authorized approval. Preserve dissent, including against Founder; record decisions and outcomes. Early R&D investment is acceptable within explicit budgets; separate operating, R&D and administrative costs. No unbounded autonomous agents.
UI: mobile, decision/workflow first, no duplicate labels or decorations; primary information by relevance, drill down to evidence and calculations; show actual data/operational health, not invented progress. Field/Lab/Office are responsibilities, not three required servers or agents.
Routine tasks are scheduled, exceptions event-driven, projects bounded by a deliverable; one accountable owner and shared records, not repeated summaries through hierarchy. External OSS/paper/strategy scouting is a Lab duty; use existing research/TRADING_OSS_RESEARCH_PROGRAM.md and promotion gates. Office can issue bounded research requests; independent validation before capital use.

## Three retrospective lessons
- Entry monitoring (#141/#142): comparison code is not an operational workflow. Inputs, versioned approvals, verified session-close data, persistence, mobile report, scheduled runs, discrepancy review and acceptance must all work.
- Portfolio decisions (#119/#120): connect proposal, approval, actual fill, reconciliation and subsequent outcome; distinguish an inferred recommendation from actual trade. No reconstruction of private balances from incomplete chat data. Reuse existing case/thesis/portfolio/risk/kernel interfaces instead of parallel canonical models.
- BNF25 (#133) and regime study (#135): missing point-in-time membership/delisting or historical series is a real blocker. Synthetic tests are not empirical validation. Register hypotheses, dataset rights, baselines, OOS splits and stopping criteria before tests; retain negative results. Do not activate unvalidated predictions.

## Development gates
G0 define the actual duty and owner; G1 prove feasibility (data rights, access, dependencies, budget, environment); G2 document SOP, input/output, exceptions, completion and golden examples; G3 implement and run tests; G4 integrate one end-to-end workflow; G5 shadow existing manual/automated operation with discrepancy log; G6 authorized acceptance and activation; G7 monitor, reconcile, audit and improve. G7 is ongoing, not a reason to delay acceptance forever.
One active Field delivery slice (currently #142); at most one bounded applied and one exploratory Lab task when separately resourced. No additional application features while the current gate is open. Blocked tasks may leave the active slot only with recorded prerequisite, alternative, owner and explicit decision; never silently substitute scope. Safety fixes may interrupt with record.
Before coding: read current issue/PR, canonical restrictions and related code; classify existing asset REUSE/ADAPT/REBUILD/ARCHIVE/BLOCKED with reason. Prefer a modular monolith, small interfaces, standard libraries, deterministic arithmetic and data transformations; AI only for materially useful uncertain reasoning with evidence, costs and limits. Do not build a generic orchestration platform before a real workflow requires it.
Inputs must preserve source, instrument, units, currency, observation/effective/availability/retrieval time when relevant, revision and validity. No hidden imputation or silently replacing missing data. Private holdings, account details, watchlists, approved private targets, credentials and restricted datasets never belong in public Git, CI logs or public artifacts.

## Definition of done
Tests executed and linked; actual data rights/accuracy verified where required; private deployment and access verified; reproducible output with versioned inputs, error path and rollback; scheduled run and actual observed results; acceptance against predefined criteria. A fixture, mock UI, successful CI, draft PR or generated report alone is not operational readiness. Research success can mean a supported rejection. Do not claim alpha from paper/synthetic/short pilot evidence.
States: PROPOSED, READY, IN_PROGRESS, BLOCKED, VERIFIED (state what was verified), PILOT, OPERATIONAL, RETIRED. No arbitrary percentages.
Each handoff: issue/PR/commit, gate, what changed, executed tests/evidence, remaining blocker, next single task, authority and rollback. Use GitHub issues/PRs for engineering only; private operations ledger separate. No production changes, merging, paid purchases, secret collection, alert cutover or order authority without appropriate approval.
