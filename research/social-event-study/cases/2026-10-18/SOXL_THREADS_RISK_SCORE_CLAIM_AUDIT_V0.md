- 단위는 화면상의 번호 하나가 아니라 **동일 작성자의 하나의 주제/스레드**로 묶는다. 번호 100개를 투자자 100명이나 독립예측 100회로 카운트하지 않는다.

## Principal finding

This is **a detailed bearish/conditional risk thesis (one author)**, not a community survey. Its historically corroborated pieces (leverage path-dependence and AI credit financing stress) are useful; the claim that a six-indicator score determines a safe entry and guaranteed max loss does not yet have data or formula support. Score model should be compared to **price-only, price+macro, price+HY credit, price+issuer credit, price+author 6 factors** and a matched market-condition baseline; veto false positive labels.
